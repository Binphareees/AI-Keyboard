# AI Keyboard (Independent Android Input Method)

A clean, modern, and privacy-conscious Android Input Method Editor (IME) built entirely from scratch in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. AI Keyboard brings next-generation artificial intelligence directly to your system keyboard, providing one-tap grammar correction, tone rewriting, multilingual translations, summarization, and smart replies across all your Android applications.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Standard Full Virtual Keyboard Engine**:
  - Full alphabetic layouts (QWERTY, Arabic RTL, Hausa with special letters).
  - Quick-switch Numbers & Symbols (`?123`) and Extended Mathematical Symbols (`=/<`).
  - Double-tap Caps Lock and Shift modes.
  - Tactile haptic feedback with configurable vibration strength and keypress sounds.
  - One-tap language switcher (`🌐`).
- **Deep AI Writing Assistant Integration**:
  - **Quick AI Action Strip**: Fix Grammar & Spelling, Polish / Rewrite, Shorten, Lengthen, Professional Tone, Casual Tone, Multilingual Translation, Summarize, Smart Reply, and Emojify.
  - **Inline AI Writing Studio**: Inspect generated diffs with instant **Replace**, **Insert**, **Copy**, **Retry**, and **Cancel** controls.
  - **Multi-Provider Architecture**: Pluggable provider system with Google Gemini 2.5, OpenAI-compatible APIs, and local fallback heuristics.
- **Privacy & Security First**:
  - Zero background keystroke logging.
  - Safe context extraction with automatic password field detection (`TYPE_TEXT_VARIATION_PASSWORD`).
  - Local heuristic processing ensures the keyboard remains fully functional offline.
- **Rich Productivity Tools**:
  - Categorized emoji picker with fast search.
  - Built-in Room database clipboard history manager.
  - 6 distinct Material 3 color themes.

---

## 🚀 How to Enable & Use

1. Build and install the APK on your Android device.
2. Open the **AI Keyboard** app from your home screen.
3. Follow the 3-step setup guide:
   - **Step 1**: Tap "Enable in Settings" and toggle on "AI Keyboard".
   - **Step 2**: Tap "Select Keyboard" and switch your default input method to "AI Keyboard".
   - **Step 3**: Try typing and testing AI actions in the interactive Playground sandbox!
